Digital twins

Why does reality disagree with the model?

A digital twin forecasts throughput that the operating system does not achieve.

The question

A digital twin forecasts throughput that the operating system does not achieve. A result alone does not tell us which mechanism produced it. Acting on an untested explanation risks optimizing the wrong thing.

What would we test?

Perturb calibration, operating conditions, and control policies; test explanations against withheld observations.

Why would anyone use this?

Target model revisions and define where predictions are dependable.

What would count as evidence?Repeated, matched comparisons; a recorded protocol and data provenance; explicit rival predictions; out-of-sample checks where feasible; and a clear statement of unresolved explanations.

Competing explanations

  1. Incorrect assumptions
  2. Environmental drift
  3. Human interventions

What you receive

A test plan, experimental comparisons, an evidence trail, limitations, and a decision-oriented conclusion.
