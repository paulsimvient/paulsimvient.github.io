Economic behavior

Why are prices unexpectedly high?

A simulated market produces prices above the expected competitive benchmark.

The question

A simulated market produces prices above the expected competitive benchmark. A result alone does not tell us which mechanism produced it. Acting on an untested explanation risks optimizing the wrong thing.

What would we test?

Run controlled market conditions with fixed agent rules and seeded comparisons; compare bids, transactions, and prices.

Why would anyone use this?

Distinguish supported response patterns from alternatives and evaluate rule changes.

What would count as evidence?Repeated, matched comparisons; a recorded protocol and data provenance; explicit rival predictions; out-of-sample checks where feasible; and a clear statement of unresolved explanations.

Competing explanations

  1. Price-history dependence
  2. Peer-action dependence
  3. Information reliability

What you receive

A test plan, experimental comparisons, an evidence trail, limitations, and a decision-oriented conclusion.
