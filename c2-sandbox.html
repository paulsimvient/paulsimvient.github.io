CoAgentics experiment layer · simulated

Now leave the historical record.

Choose one explanation. CoAgentics constructs a matched intervention while keeping the scenario seed and other conditions fixed. The output below is illustrative sandbox output—not an NTSB finding.

Sources & limits

What is real, and what is modeled?

  • NTSB Aviation Investigation Report AIR-26-02 — final report, including impact altitude and altitude-system findings.
  • NTSB Preliminary Report DCA25MA108 — time sequence, 6.5 nm and 0.95 nm separation, CRJ radio altitude/speed, and radio-transmission details.
  • NTSB Hearing Animation Description — reconstruction sources: FDR, CVR, radar, ADS-B, ATC, and surveillance video.

This public web demo is an educational/research visualization. It does not reproduce the complete NTSB animation, CVR audio, or every flight-data sample. The 3D scene uses schematic spatial reconstruction while the numeric case facts above are explicitly labeled and sourced.
