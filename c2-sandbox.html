Sources & limits

What is real, and what is modeled?

  • NTSB Aviation Investigation Report AIR-26-02 — final report, including impact altitude and altitude-system findings.
  • NTSB Preliminary Report DCA25MA108 — time sequence, separations, CRJ radio altitude/speed, radio-transmission details.
  • NTSB Hearing Animation Description — reconstruction sources: FDR, CVR, radar, ADS-B, ATC, and surveillance video.

Educational/research visualization. Matched experiment paths are a schematic toy model on this stage. NTSB reports remain the authority on the accident.
