Where would you use CoAgentics?

Use it when the outcome is visible but the cause is uncertain—and choosing the wrong explanation could lead to the wrong decision.

Choose a problem worth investigating.

Each case starts with an observed problem, sets out rival explanations, proposes a discriminating experiment, and explains the decision it could inform.
