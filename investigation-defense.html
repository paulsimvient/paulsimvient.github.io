Autonomous operations

Why did the mission plan break down?

An autonomous team loses performance when communications become unreliable.

The question

An autonomous team loses performance when communications become unreliable. A result alone does not tell us which mechanism produced it. Acting on an untested explanation risks optimizing the wrong thing.

What would we test?

Replay matched scenarios under controlled communication and policy interventions; compare mission outcomes and traces.

Why would anyone use this?

Find operating limits and prioritize robust coordination changes.

What would count as evidence?Repeated, matched comparisons; a recorded protocol and data provenance; explicit rival predictions; out-of-sample checks where feasible; and a clear statement of unresolved explanations.

Competing explanations

  1. Coordination latency
  2. Missing observations
  3. Policy fragility

What you receive

A test plan, experimental comparisons, an evidence trail, limitations, and a decision-oriented conclusion.
