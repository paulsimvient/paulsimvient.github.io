The book

Thinking Together

How Humans and AI Build Understanding Together

What happens when AI becomes part of how we think — not just how quickly we get answers?

Thinking Together is about keeping inquiry intact when language gets smooth. Use AI to observe carefully, hold competing explanations, test one change at a time, check the evidence, and carry the limits forward.

Otherwise the edges blur: a qualified result becomes an unqualified sentence; dependent reports become “multiple sources”; a simulation becomes “what will happen”; a hypothesis becomes “the cause.” The book keeps those edges in view.

The inquiry loop

Observe → Frame → Test → Validate → Return

Return matters because good inquiry should change what a person notices next time.
