Thinking Together

How Humans and AI Build Understanding Together

Language models are persuasive compressors. They can turn partial evidence into finished-sounding explanations. The book asks how people can use AI without losing the distinctions that inquiry depends on.

The method is concrete: Observe. Frame. Test. Validate. Return. The aim is to make evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, and responsibility visible long enough for people to learn from the process.
