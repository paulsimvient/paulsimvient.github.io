The book

Thinking Together

How Humans and AI Build Understanding Together

What happens when AI becomes part of how we think — not just how quickly we get answers?

Thinking Together argues that the best use of AI is to help people observe carefully, keep competing explanations alive, test ideas, check evidence, and carry what they learn into the next problem.

Fluency can erase the distinctions that inquiry depends on.

A qualified result can become an unqualified sentence. Dependent reports can become “multiple sources.” A simulation can become “what will happen.” A hypothesis can become “the cause.” The book keeps those distinctions visible.

The inquiry loop

Observe → Frame → Test → Validate → Return

Return matters because good inquiry should change what a person notices next time.
