About

Paul Cummings, Ph.D.

Author of Thinking Together — opening the AI black box through observation, experimentation, and discovery.

Paul Cummings is a researcher and senior technologist. His work focuses on modeling and simulation, agent-based modeling, artificial intelligence, and computational social science. He earned his Ph.D. in Computational Social Science from George Mason University. His dissertation examined machine-learning and agent-based approaches to decision-making heuristics.

Across nearly three decades, that work has centered on using modeling and simulation to study complex systems, human behavior, decision making, and opaque computational systems. He is an advisor to George Mason University’s Fuse Center. His interests there include methods for probing AI behavior through inspectable inquiry.
