About

Modeling systems that can be questioned.

Paul Cummings, Ph.D. works across agent-based simulation, multi-agent AI, computational social science, and experimental validation. His focus is the design of computational environments where hypotheses, interventions, evidence, uncertainty, and human judgment remain inspectable.

Evidence systems

Preserve provenance, uncertainty, contradiction, holdouts, and validity limits.
