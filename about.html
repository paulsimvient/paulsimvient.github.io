Paul Cummings, Ph.D.

Modeling, simulation, computational social science, and the design of systems that help humans and machines investigate complex problems together.

PC

Paul Cummings is a researcher and senior advisor working at the intersection of modeling and simulation, agent-based systems, multi-agent AI, and experimental validation.

He earned his Ph.D. in Computational Social Science from George Mason University. His dissertation examined decision-making heuristics using a hybrid of machine learning and agent-based modeling.

His current work explores how AI can participate in inquiry without hiding the evidence, uncertainty, and human judgment that give conclusions their meaning. He is also involved with the George Mason University Fuse Center and works with organizations applying computational methods to national-security and other complex decision environments.

The aim is not faster answers. It is better inquiry, better judgment, and learning that survives the interaction.
