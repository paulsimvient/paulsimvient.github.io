About

Paul Cummings, Ph.D.

Researcher and senior technologist focused on making complex systems — including opaque AI — something people can investigate rather than merely trust.

The work sits at the intersection of agent-based simulation, multi-agent AI, computational social science, and experimental validation. The through-line is practical: build environments where assumptions, tests, evidence, uncertainty, and human judgment stay visible.

MODELS

Simulations you can question

Represent actors, rules, and outcomes so interventions and alternatives remain explicit.

TESTS

Comparisons you can inspect

Design controlled and counterfactual comparisons that separate competing explanations.

EVIDENCE

Records you can keep

Retain provenance, uncertainty, contradictions, and the limits of what was shown.
