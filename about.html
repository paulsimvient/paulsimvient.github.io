About

Paul Cummings, Ph.D.

Researcher and senior technologist working on inspectable AI and simulation — so teams can test claims instead of taking fluency at face value.

The work sits at the intersection of agent-based simulation, multi-agent systems, computational social science, and experimental validation. Engagements typically look like evaluation design, modeling environments where assumptions stay explicit, and teaching cases that walk a team through matched comparisons.

MODELS

Simulations you can question

Represent actors, rules, and outcomes so interventions and alternatives remain explicit.

TESTS

Comparisons you can inspect

Design controlled and counterfactual comparisons that separate competing explanations.

EVIDENCE

Records you can keep

Retain provenance, uncertainty, contradictions, and the limits of what was shown.
