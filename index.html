How an investigation stays inspectable

Six jobs. One evidence trail.

Instead of accepting a fluent answer, keep each part of the investigation separate enough that a person can see what was assumed, what was tested, and what remains open.

01 / WORLD

Fix what matters

Say what is in the situation: observations, actions, constraints, and outcomes.

02 / ALTERNATIVES

Keep rivals alive

Do not collapse early to one story. Carry competing explanations long enough to test them.

03 / TEST

Compare under control

Change one thing at a time. Run a matched comparison so the difference means something.

04 / CHECK

Ask what survives

Separate exploration from confirmation. Reserve a final check the first pass never saw.

05 / MEMORY

Keep the limits

Store the claim with its evidence, contradictions, uncertainty, and boundaries.

06 / HUMAN REVIEW

People stay in charge

Inspect, challenge, approve, or reject. Fluency never becomes authority by itself.
