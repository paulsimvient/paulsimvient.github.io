The method

Evidence before confidence.

Frame the decision

Identify the mission outcome, system boundaries, and assumptions that matter.

Separate explanations

Use controlled comparisons to distinguish competing accounts of observed behavior.

Validate the claim

Check predictions against new conditions and make uncertainty visible.
