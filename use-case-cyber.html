The mission problem

What needs to be understood?

A mission network becomes unreliable, but alerts do not establish the cause.

Where this applies

Cyber defenders and mission owners need to distinguish malicious activity from configuration changes, load, equipment faults, and upstream dependencies.

Experimental inquiry

What would CoAgentics test?

  1. Which observed behaviors are consistent with each proposed failure mechanism?
  2. Can controlled fault injection or replay distinguish competing explanations?
  3. Which containment action restores performance while preserving essential operations?

CoAgentics would frame competing explanations, design matched comparisons, and test predictions under controlled conditions rather than infer causes from a single observed outcome.

Evidence outputs

What the team would receive

  • Service availability and recovery time
  • Latency, error rates, and dependency health
  • Response tradeoffs and operational impact
  • Residual uncertainty after testing

A traceable assessment of supported explanations, alternative explanations, uncertainty, and recommended follow-up tests.
