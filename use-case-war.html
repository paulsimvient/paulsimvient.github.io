The mission problem

What needs to be understood?

A mission-critical capability falls short of readiness requirements despite apparently sufficient resources.

Where this applies

Commanders, program managers, logistics planners, and mission analysts need to understand which dependencies affect readiness and which interventions can improve outcomes.

Experimental inquiry

What would CoAgentics test?

  1. Is the shortfall driven by maintenance capacity, supply availability, training throughput, or coordination?
  2. Does the limiting factor change under surge demand or degraded communications?
  3. Which mitigation improves mission availability without creating another bottleneck?

CoAgentics would frame competing explanations, design matched comparisons, and test predictions under controlled conditions rather than infer causes from a single observed outcome.

Evidence outputs

What the team would receive

  • Mission availability and recovery time
  • Maintenance and supply lead times
  • Personnel and platform utilization
  • Sensitivity to operational constraints

A traceable assessment of supported explanations, alternative explanations, uncertainty, and recommended follow-up tests.
