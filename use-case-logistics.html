Global logistics

Test AI models. Understand global networks. Improve operational decisions.

Worldwide logistics connects ports, shipping lanes, air cargo, rail, suppliers, distribution centers, and markets. AI forecasts and optimization systems influence routing, capacity, inventory, and response to disruption. CoAgentics tests those models and their recommendations under controlled conditions to establish where decisions remain effective—and where assumptions fail.

The global mission

A worldwide decision system, not a single port

A disruption in one region can shift demand, routes, inventories, and capacity across continents. Decisions that optimize one node may make the overall network less resilient. The goal is to test AI-supported decisions across the entire system—not simply explain what happened locally.

Where AI enters the mission

Models under investigation

Forecasting and early warning

Do demand, ETA, congestion, and disruption models retain predictive accuracy when trade patterns or operating conditions shift?

Routing and resource optimization

Do AI-recommended routes, schedules, capacity assignments, and inventory placements actually improve total-network outcomes?

Multi-agent behavior

What happens when carriers, suppliers, customers, and automated decision systems respond to one another, potentially shifting congestion elsewhere?

Human–AI decisions

When should operators follow, challenge, or override recommendations? How should uncertainty be communicated?

Controlled investigation

Test the model—not just the forecast

  1. Establish baseline: measure network flow, cost, service levels, and model predictions.
  2. Introduce interventions: change port capacity, trade restrictions, weather, demand, or transportation availability.
  3. Compare alternatives: evaluate AI recommendations against simpler baselines and competing models under matched scenarios.
  4. Measure tradeoffs: delivery time, total cost, resilience, recovery time, and worst-case outcomes.
  5. Validate: freeze model recommendations before testing held-out scenarios and report limitations.

Evidence outputs

What the customer receives

  • Model-by-model predictive and decision performance
  • Scenario-specific failure and sensitivity boundaries
  • Tradeoffs across cost, time, resilience, and risk
  • Reproducible interventions and competing explanations
  • Supported recommendations, uncertainty, and next tests
CoAgentics evaluates which choices are better supported under defined objectives and constraints; it does not promise a universally optimal solution.
