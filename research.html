Architecture

Inspectable by construction.

CoAgentics treats AI analysis as a controlled computational process. Each layer has a distinct job, and evidence moves through explicit interfaces.

Matched counterfactuals

Where possible, baseline and intervention runs share exogenous randomness. This reduces irrelevant run-to-run variation and makes the contrast easier to interpret.

Discovery stays separate from confirmation

Candidate models, thresholds, exclusions, and stopping rules can evolve during discovery. Before confirmation they are frozen. Reserved conditions remain inaccessible to the selector and classifier until validation.

DISCOVERY

Learn what to test

Explore candidate explanations and choose discriminating interventions.

FREEZE

Lock the protocol

Freeze model set, metrics, exclusions, thresholds, and holdout definition.

CONFIRM

Test what survives

Run independent reserved conditions and report both support and failure.

Evidence is a first-class object

Results are retained with provenance. A claim is never just a sentence. It carries source configuration, experiment identity, treatment, seed, model version, uncertainty, contradictions, and stated limits.

{ claim_id, status, evidence_refs, counterevidence_refs, uncertainty, validity_envelope, provenance }

AI does not acquire authority by sounding certain

Conversational models may organize, summarize, compare, and explain. They do not select or authorize operational action. That boundary is architectural, not a prompt convention.
