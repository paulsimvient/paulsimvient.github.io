Method

Make the investigation inspectable.

For teams evaluating AI or simulation claims: systems can sound finished while remaining opaque. Keep competing explanations visible, run controlled comparisons, and retain what the evidence actually supports.

Six jobs Fair comparisons Explore, then confirm Keep the limits What this is not

Six jobs in one evidence trail

Each job stays separate enough that a person can see what was assumed, what was tested, and what remains open.

Fair comparisons

Where possible, the unchanged case and the changed case share the same setup. That makes the difference easier to interpret: only the intended change should explain the gap.

Explore, then confirm

During exploration, explanations and tests can evolve. Before the final check, lock the rules. Keep a reserved case the first pass never used.

EXPLORE

Learn what to test

Carry rivals and choose comparisons that should separate them.

LOCK

Freeze the standard

Lock which explanations count and what confirmation requires.

CONFIRM

Test what survives

Use reserved conditions and report both support and failure.

Keep the limits with the claim

A result is never just a sentence. It carries what was tested, what conflicted, how uncertain it is, and where it stops applying.

What this is not

This is not a chatbot that decides for you. Conversational models may help organize and explain. They do not acquire authority by sounding certain. People remain responsible for what becomes an operational conclusion.
