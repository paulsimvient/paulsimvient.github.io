Consulting

Understand complex systems.
Test what works.

Research advisory, computational modeling, and experimental validation for AI, defense, and complex decision environments.

I help organizations understand how intelligent systems behave, where their assumptions fail, and what evidence supports their use in consequential decisions.

My work combines computational social science, agent-based simulation, multi-agent AI, and controlled experimentation. The objective is not simply to build more sophisticated models. It is to learn what they can reliably tell us.

What an engagement produces

Work begins with a specific question, a baseline, and an explicit account of what evidence would change the conclusion. Deliverables are tailored to the research problem.

Research plan

Testable questions, competing explanations, assumptions, measures, and acceptance criteria.

Experimental evidence

Simulation or evaluation protocols, reproducible comparisons, uncertainty estimates, and failure cases.

Decision-ready findings

A concise technical report, working prototype or research roadmap that distinguishes supported claims from open questions.

How I can help

AI Evaluation & Black-Box Research

Understand what AI systems actually do.

Large language models can produce convincing explanations without revealing the processes behind their behavior. I design experiments that investigate observable behavior, compare competing explanations, and identify where model performance changes.

  • Black-box AI evaluation and behavioral testing
  • Multi-agent and LLM experimentation
  • Counterfactual analysis and controlled comparisons
  • Model reliability, uncertainty, and evidence validation

Computational Modeling & Digital Twins

Explore systems that are difficult to study directly.

Agent-based models and simulation environments make it possible to examine interactions, test interventions, and explore outcomes under controlled conditions.

Applications include economic markets, aviation, autonomous systems, defense operations, and organizational behavior.

  • Agent-based modeling and simulation
  • Synthetic environments and digital twins
  • Scenario design and sensitivity analysis
  • Human–AI interaction and emergent behavior

CoAgentics: a research workbench

CoAgentics brings modeling, strategy, experimentation, validation, and human inquiry into a common environment.

Rather than accepting an AI-generated explanation, researchers can compare hypotheses, change assumptions, run controlled experiments, and examine the evidence.

The goal is to make the behavior of complex systems more understandable — and the limits of that understanding visible.
