Two aircraft. One corridor.
Many explanations.

Near Reagan National on the evening of Jan. 29, 2025, a CRJ and an Army helicopter collided. This page is not a verdict. It is a description of the teaching case — then a door into a sandbox where you can keep rival explanations alive and test them under matched conditions.

Run Sandbox → How the method works →
What this case is for

Use the record first. Then change one thing.

CoAgentics treats the historical sequence as fixed evidence, then crosses a bright line into simulation. In the sandbox you reconstruct the encounter, pick one hypothesis, and run thousands of matched trials — same world noise, one controlled change — to see what the counterfactual creates.

01

Historical record

Traffic advisory, visual separation, TCAS, pass-behind, final, impact at 278 ft MSL — sourced from NTSB material.

02

Rival explanations

Altitude perception, advisory comprehension, and encounter timing stay visible as competing claims.

03

Matched counterfactuals

Each trial shares a seed family. Only the selected factor moves. Rank which change mattered most.

What you will see in the sandbox

Baseline vs counterfactual

Two paths on one stage. The counterfactual is what the intervention creates — a raised route, a divert, or a time shift — not a second historical claim.

Live tracks + radar

Derived lat/lon, altitude, heading, and a sweeping radar scope tied to craft motion. Geometry is schematic; numbers that are recorded stay labeled.

Auto trial loop

Hypotheses cycle automatically at 10× playback. Each run re-samples matched trials and states what changed and what it created.
