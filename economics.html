Can we tell what moves a trader’s bid?

Use a controlled 2×2 information design. Hold private value fixed, vary news and price history, score rival models, and check a sealed holdout cell.

Run the worked example →
1. Name the policy 2. Control what is seen 3. Score rival stories 4. Seal a holdout 5. Unlock and compare 6. Keep the record

1. Name the policy first

The worked example uses an explicit toy rule: bid = 75 + 5·H + 3·N + 12·H·N + ε under seed 42. Every number on the page comes from that function — not from a hidden AI.

2. Control what the policy can see

Private value stays fixed at $100. Information factors change on purpose: price history (stable vs rising) and news (none vs positive).

3. Score rival behavioral stories

Compare observable models: news only, history only, or an interaction. Rank them by SSE on discovery-cell means — computed, not scripted.

4. Seal one cell until freeze

Three discovery cells identify a candidate. Positive news · rising stays sealed until you freeze the claim.

5. Unlock and compare predictions

Reveal the holdout mean. Compare it to the additive prediction and the interaction prediction. If rivals still look the same, say so.

6. Keep the full evidence record

Retain the policy, seed, trial count, contrasts, SSE ranks, and holdout errors with the candidate claim.
