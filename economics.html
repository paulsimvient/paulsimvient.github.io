Case study / Economics

Behavior under controlled market pressure.

Use a dynamic market as an experimental instrument. Control what an opaque agent can see, vary information and incentives, compare predictive models, and test which explanation survives.

1. Qualify the market before testing the AI

The environment tracks private buyer values and seller costs, cash, inventory, bids, asks, trades, surplus, and allocative efficiency. Known-rule agents verify accounting and mechanism behavior before unfamiliar models are introduced.

Private statevalue / cost / inventory / cash
Marketcontinuous or sealed-bid double auction
Actiontime / asset / quantity / price
Outcometrades / surplus / efficiency / price

2. Separate true state from observed state

The true economic state stays fixed while the observation shown to the agent changes. News reliability, timing, wording, price history, and peer visibility become controlled factors rather than loose context.

Held constantVaried experimentally
fundamental value, accounts, quantity constraintsnews, market history, peer observations

3. Carry rival behavioral models

The system does not infer hidden psychology. It compares observable predictive models: price-history dependence, news sensitivity, peer-action dependence, persistent shading, or interactions among these factors.

H1: bid ~ private_value + news
H2: bid ~ private_value + price_history
H3: bid ~ private_value + news × price_history

4. Choose the experiment that best separates them

A fixed battery can waste calls. During discovery, the selector can score feasible interventions by predicted model separation divided by experiment cost. Reserved confirmation conditions remain off limits.

e* = argmax D[P(Y|H1,e), P(Y|H2,e)] / C(e)

5. Freeze, hold out, confirm

After discovery, the model set, outcomes, thresholds, exclusions, sample size, and stopping logic are frozen. Confirmation changes valuations, costs, history, news, mechanism, observation format, or interacting-agent conditions that were not used to choose the model.

If two explanations remain observationally equivalent, the result is non-identifiable.

6. Retain the full evidence record

Each trial retains experiment identity, seed, model configuration, exact observation, raw response where permitted, validated action, latency, submitted order, trades, account changes, market metrics, and whether the run belongs to discovery or confirmation.
