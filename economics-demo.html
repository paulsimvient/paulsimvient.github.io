Matched tests

Where possible, treatment and comparison runs share the same seed so irrelevant randomness cancels out.

Holdout

Discovery conditions identify a candidate pattern. Unseen conditions are kept sealed until the model and stopping rule are frozen.

What we retain

Not “the AI thinks X.” We retain the tested behavioral claim, supporting evidence, uncertainty, and the conditions where it may fail.
